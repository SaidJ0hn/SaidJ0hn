Navbat Privacy Policy

Effective: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Navbat online booking service collects, why, where and for how long it is stored, who can see it, and how you can control it. If anything is unclear, contact us (see section 13).

1. Who we are

Navbat is an online booking service in Telegram for salons, barbershops and other service businesses. It works through the businesses' Telegram bots and Mini Apps and through the owner console bot @Navbatci_bot.

Service operator: [sole proprietor / LLC "name"], TIN [TIN], address: [registered address], Republic of Uzbekistan ("Navbat", "we").

This policy follows the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" No. ZRU-547 of 2 July 2019 and other Uzbek legislation.

2. Who owns the data

  • Clients book services at a business. The client base belongs to the business: the business is the owner of this personal data, and Navbat processes it on the business's behalf and only to run the service.
  • Staff (the business's masters) use the staff app. Navbat processes their data on the business's behalf.
  • Business owners and administrators use the @Navbatci_bot console. Navbat processes their data as owner, to perform its contract with the business.

Each business is responsible for its own services, prices, client communication and broadcasts. Navbat never sells or shares one business's client base with another.

3.2. Staff and owners

Name, Telegram ID, phone, photo (if uploaded), position, work schedule, services, branches; for owners also the business name and address, plan and subscription payment history.

3.3. Technical data

IP address, device and browser type, request times and errors, recorded in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

3.4. What we do not collect

  • payment card data: clients do not pay through Navbat, and businesses pay for the subscription outside the service;
  • passport data, biometrics, health, religion or other special categories of data;
  • your location or your phone's contact list;
  • your Telegram chats, other than messages you send to the bot itself.

Please do not put sensitive information (for example about your health) into booking comments or reviews.

4. Why we use data

  1. To create a booking and show it to you, the staff member and the business administrator.
  2. To send booking notifications: confirmation, reminder, reschedule, cancellation, review request.
  3. To run the waitlist and tell you when a slot opens.
  4. To let the business keep its client records, visit history and statistics.
  5. To send the business's news and offers, unless you have opted out (see section 5).
  6. To protect the service from abuse: fake bookings, spam, intrusion.
  7. To fix errors and improve the service using anonymised statistics.
  8. To comply with the law (for example, keeping accounting records of business payments).

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, do not sell it and do not build advertising profiles.

5. Legal basis and consent

We process data based on your consent, to perform a contract (a booking or a business subscription), and where the law requires it.

A client gives consent by starting the business's bot and confirming a booking, and separately for the phone number by tapping "Share phone number". You may withdraw consent at any time (section 9). We will then stop processing, but we cannot accept new bookings from you, because a booking is impossible without data.

Broadcasts. A bot can send you a business's offers only because you started a conversation with it. You can opt out at any time in the Mini App settings or with the /stop command. Notifications about your own bookings will still be sent.

6. Who can see your data

  • The business you booked with: its administrators see your name, phone, visit history and reviews at that business only.
  • The staff member sees only bookings with them: name, service, time, comment and, if needed, phone.
  • Other clients see only published reviews: a shortened name, the rating and the text. Your phone number and Telegram ID are never shown to other clients.
  • Navbat staff, only when necessary: to answer a support request, fix a failure or comply with the law. Access is restricted and logged.
  • Telegram, through which the bot and Mini App work. Telegram is an independent platform that processes data under its own policy (telegram.org/privacy).
  • Hosting provider [name], under contract, bound by confidentiality, with no right to use the data for its own purposes.
  • Public authorities, only on a lawful request and to the extent the law requires.

If we add payment providers in the future (for example Payme or Click), we will update this policy first.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (name and phone can be changed in the Mini App profile);
  • delete your data: "Delete my data" in the Mini App profile, or a request to us;
  • withdraw consent and opt out of broadcasts;
  • complain to the authorised state body for personal data of the Republic of Uzbekistan or to a court.

We answer requests within 10 business days. To protect you from fraud, we may ask you to confirm that the request comes from your Telegram account. Clients may also contact the business directly; we will help it fulfil the request.

When a profile is deleted, future bookings are cancelled and past bookings are anonymised: the business keeps the visit and amount for its records, without your name or contacts.

10. How we protect data

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Every Mini App request is verified with Telegram's digital signature, so nobody can impersonate your account.
  • Access is role-based: a business sees only its own clients, staff only their own bookings, a branch only its own branch unless the owner grants more.
  • Access keys and bot tokens are kept separately from code in protected storage.
  • Backups are encrypted.
  • Navbat staff access is limited to the minimum necessary and logged.
  • Rate limiting and blocking of suspicious activity protect against mass requests and brute force.
  • Security updates are applied regularly.

No system on the internet is perfectly secure. If a breach may affect your rights, we will notify the affected businesses and users as soon as possible and notify the authorised body as required by law.

Please protect your Telegram account too: turn on two-step verification and never share login codes.

11. Children

The service is not intended for independent use by children under 16. If a parent books for a child, the parent enters and controls the child's data. If you believe a child gave us data without parental consent, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Browser storage

The Mini App stores only your settings (language and theme) on your device. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.
